    How to play (in 1 minute)

    1. Goal: make 4 sets + 1 pair (14 tiles). A set is a Pung (3 identical, e.g. ) or a Chow (3 in a row of one suit, e.g. ). A Kong is 4 identical (counts as a set; you draw a bonus tile).
    2. Your turn: you get a new tile (shown on the right). Tap a tile to pick it, tap again to throw it away.
    3. Grab a discard: when someone throws a tile you can use, big buttons pop up. Win beats Pung/Kong, which beats Chow. Only the next player can Chow.
    4. Win! Press it when your hand is complete - from a discard or a tile you drew yourself (self-drawn).
    5. Special hands: Seven Pairs, and Thirteen Orphans (one of every 1, 9, wind and dragon + any pair).
    6. Flowers are set aside automatically and give bonus points.
    7. Stuck? Press Hint - it glows the tile to throw. "Ready!" means you're one tile away from winning.

    Scoring (faan): self-drawn 1 · concealed hand 1 · all chows 1 · dragon or your-wind pung 1 · all pungs 3 · half flush 3 · full flush 7 · seven pairs 4 · thirteen orphans 13 (max). Points = 2faan; the thrower pays double, or everyone pays on self-draw.
